# 🏦 Bank Loan Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Goal
To develop a comprehensive, three-page interactive Power BI dashboard to monitor the health and performance of the bank's loan portfolio, enabling data-driven decisions on credit risk management and lending strategy.

## 📊 Key Results & Impact
* **Credit Risk:** Identified an overall **Bad Loan Rate of 13.82%**, isolating high-risk exposure in the 60-month term products and Grade F/G loans.
* **Funding Volume:** Tracked **$435.8 Million** in Total Funded Amount, with detailed trends by month, purpose, and home ownership status.
* **Profitability:** Visualized the relationship between Funded Amount and Total Amount Received to assess overall portfolio revenue.

## 🛠️ Technical Skills Demonstrated
* **Data Modeling:** Used Power Query to connect, cleanse, and structure pre-aggregated and detail-level CSV data.
* **DAX:** Developed crucial analytical measures, including `Bad Loan %` and `Total Amount Received`, to quantify performance.
* **Visualization:** Designed a three-page dark-themed report (Summary, Overview, Details) using Cards, Line Charts, Treemaps, and custom Slicers.
